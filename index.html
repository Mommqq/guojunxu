About

I am a direct-entry Ph.D. student at Wuhan University of Technology, advised by Prof. Junwei Zhou. I received my B.S. degree in Information and Computing Science from Wuhan University of Technology in 2024.

My work studies distributed image compression at extremely low bitrates, with an emphasis on decoder-side information, generative priors, perceptual quality, and target-image fidelity.
